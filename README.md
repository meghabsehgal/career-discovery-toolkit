# Career Discovery & Life Design Toolkit by Megha

Welcome to my personal toolkit for career discovery and intentional life design. This repository is a living space where I’m documenting my journey of uncovering what energizes me, how I define success, and how I build experiments to align with my values and strengths.

This toolkit is open-source and designed for **multi-passionate, reflective professionals** who want more than just a job title — they want purpose, flexibility, and joy in how they work and live.

---

## 📘 1. Career Discovery Worksheet

This worksheet helps you reflect on:
- What energizes you
- Personal victories and moments of pride
- Topics you’re naturally drawn to
- Work environments where you thrive
- Desired lifestyle, income, and day structure
- Clues from outside work (hobbies, parenting, healing, etc.)

## 📆 2. Weekly Reflection Template

A 20-minute journaling guide to check in with yourself:

```markdown
### Weekly Reflection
- What gave me energy this week?
- What drained me?
- What did I avoid, and why?
- One thing I did that I’m proud of:
- One thing I want to change next week:
```

Use this to build awareness and course-correct in small steps.

## 🎯 3. Goal-Setting Flow for Multi-Passionate People

Many of us have more than one interest — and that’s a gift.
This section helps you channel that energy with intention:

```markdown
### Quarterly Focus Map
- What are my 3 areas of focus this quarter?
- What does progress look like in each?
- What’s my "enough" goal vs. stretch goal?
- Which of these will I experiment with publicly (learning-in-public)?
```
